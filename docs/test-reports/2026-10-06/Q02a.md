## Six bounded behavior contracts

1. Binding is adjacent and single-use. Only a successful explicit unique-hero
   answer can establish it. Every next ask consumes the previous binding first,
   including exceptions, unsupported syntax and intervening turns. A successful
   pronoun follow-up does not renew it. Deterministic conflict/refusal/invalid
   citations never establish a binding. Known refusal wording is conservatively
   rejected even with valid citations; this is not a general semantic classifier.
2. The actual citation gate must pass and at least one actually cited hero
   entry must have the explicitly asked canonical identity. Any other actually
   cited hero identity prevents acceptance. Merely retrieved candidates, lineup
   members, generated prose and externally supplied history cannot establish it.
   Aliases must resolve uniquely. Q03a partial caused by notes remains eligible.
3. Acceptance binds the exact history list object, its complete ordered
   role/content/evidence-ID fingerprint, and the completed user/assistant pair
   position. Reset, replacement, clear, order/content changes and malformed turn
   contents invalidate it. This cannot detect a mutation fully undone between
   calls; no such promise is made. Calls are assumed serial, not concurrent.
4. A white-listed follow-up deep-copies current `Retriever.entries` and builds
   a new Retriever/index. Raw gating, resolved retrieval and Q03a use that same
   owned snapshot. Existing stale index state is not used. Every actually cited
   previous entry has a complete model-content fingerprint, including identity,
   source, notes and metadata. Changes/withdrawals invalidate the binding.
   This refreshes the current in-memory catalog, not disk. Newly added or
   previously uncited same-hero evidence is not filtered by old citation IDs.
5. Any non-None images argument clears and cannot establish the text binding,
   then follows the legacy image path. No image is treated as Q02a evidence.
   Zero-model guarantees here apply only to text-only white-listed branches.
6. White-list handling is terminal. Raw miss keeps the original no-evidence
   answer and zero rewrite/answer calls. With raw evidence but no valid binding,
   or empty/wrong-subject resolved evidence, return `请明确指出要查询的武将名称。`
   with zero rewrite/answer calls. No fallback to LLM rewrite. Only fresh resolved
   target-hero chunks survive into final evidence; Q03a and the real prompt use
   the resolved question, while history retains the original user wording.

Grammar is full-match: `他|该武将`, optional `的`, one of
`初始|基础|满级|成长`, one of `武力|智力|统率|先攻`, optional `是`, `多少`,
and at most one trailing `？`, `?`, or `。`. Leading/trailing whitespace is
stripped. Internal whitespace, extra clauses, plural, comparisons, conditions,
seasons and general follow-ups are outside the grammar. They retain the legacy
path while consuming the old binding. No general Q02/NLU claim is made.

## Versioned evaluation and limits

Default remains v1. v1/v2 fixtures and historical reports are immutable and
reject the new production tree. v3 inherits the original query/top-k/labels,
scoring definitions and scalar cases unchanged, adding a separate required
nonempty, strictly shaped multi-turn suite. Version is strict integer, top-k
strict positive integer, IDs nonempty/unique; source-add/delete/content drift
and imported-source-root rejection stay enabled.

The 9/9 multi-turn denominator counts developer-authored fake-client scenarios:
alias success, absent binding, wrong citation, raw miss, single-use expiry,
source withdrawal, new conflict, history replacement and notes partial.
It is neither independent gold nor provider answer quality. The original
retrieval Recall/MRR remains 10/11 over the 11 answerable queries out of 12.

Returned evidence is still top-k capped, not a whole-KB contradiction proof.
Full semantic entailment/refusal detection, arbitrary pronouns, images, complete
Q02, fresh disk governance, Q05/Q08, human gold, independent holdout, real provider
quality and production readiness remain unaccepted. Independent CR must review
the exact final code SHA above; author green tests do not substitute for approval.
