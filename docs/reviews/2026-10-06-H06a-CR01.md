## HIGH / P1 finding

Location: `packages/pioneer-agent/src/pioneer_agent/app/game_agent.py:180-197`.

A fake managed client's call raises `asyncio.CancelledError`; its `__aexit__` raises a separate `RuntimeError`. The runner persists cancellation and propagates it, but the surrounding `async with client` replaces the primary exception with the cleanup error. The generic CLI handler sees only `RuntimeError` and returns a regular result dictionary instead of propagating task cancellation. This violates H06a's explicit primary-error/cancellation preservation gate, and the caller's cancellation contract is lost precisely during failing cleanup. The required correction is to retain the body failure/cancellation independently of cleanup failure, retain both diagnostically, and keep ownership until cleanup is finished. Do not treat this finding as permission to change unrelated transport/runtime behavior.
